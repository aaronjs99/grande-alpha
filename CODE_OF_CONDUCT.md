# Community code of conduct

Participate with respect, patience, and technical honesty. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish another person's private information, encourage reckless financial behavior, or misrepresent simulated results as real returns.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, deceptive, unsafe, privacy-invasive, or off-topic contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement reports require a private contact before the public community opens; that contact is a release-blocking checklist item.
