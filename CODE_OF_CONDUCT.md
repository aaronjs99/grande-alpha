# Community code of conduct

## Our standard

GRANDE Alpha welcomes respectful, patient, and technically honest participation. Examples of
constructive behavior include giving actionable feedback, acknowledging uncertainty, respecting
different experience levels, and separating simulated evidence from real-world claims.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal
attacks, publishing private information, encouraging reckless financial behavior, misrepresenting
results, or using project channels to solicit money or promote financial products.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces where
someone is representing the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, deceptive, unsafe, privacy-invasive, or off-topic content
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should consider
context, severity, repetition, and the safety of affected participants.

For ordinary moderation, flag the relevant public content without repeating sensitive material. For
a confidential report, use the repository's
[private Security Advisory form](https://github.com/aaronjs99/grande-alpha/security/advisories/new)
and prefix the title with `[conduct]`. Do not include broker credentials, account information, or
unrelated financial records. Response times and confidentiality outcomes cannot be guaranteed.
